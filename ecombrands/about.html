01 About Ecom Brands LLC · Newark, Delaware

A company built around how people find things now.

Ecom Brands LLC is a U.S.-based consumer e-commerce company focused on building and operating digitally native consumer businesses.

Incorporated in Delaware. Operating internationally. Quiet about the portfolio, open about the method.

02 The company Plainly stated

We own the
brands we
sell.

Ecom Brands identifies consumer product opportunities, builds brands around them, creates the content that makes people want them, and operates the direct-to-consumer storefronts where those products are sold.

That is the whole company. We are not an agency and we do not sell services. There is no client sitting behind us. When you work with Ecom Brands you are dealing with the brand owner — we place the orders, we hold the inventory decisions, we run the storefronts and we carry the commercial risk.

We keep the company small and the loop tight on purpose. Content is produced in-house rather than outsourced, because the people deciding what to sell should be the same people finding out what consumers respond to.

04 Operating principles Four, and we mean them
P—01

Build the brand before the broadcast.

Anyone can put a product in front of people. Very few products survive being seen. Naming, packaging, price architecture and storefront come first, because attention arriving at an unfinished brand is attention wasted — and it only arrives once.

P—02

Earn attention rather than rent it.

Paid distribution stops the day the budget stops. Organic distribution keeps working. So the question we ask about every piece of content is not “will this convert?” but “would anyone choose to watch this?” The second question is harder and worth far more.

P—03

The product has to survive the second look.

Content creates the first purchase. The product decides everything after it — the review, the repeat order, whether the brand is worth keeping. This is why manufacturing quality is a commercial decision for us, not a procurement detail.

P—04

Partners are infrastructure, not vendors.

A brand that grows quickly puts real pressure on the people who make and ship it. We would rather build a small number of long relationships with partners who can hold that pressure than run a constant search for the lowest quote.
