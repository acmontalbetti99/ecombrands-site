01 Who we are looking for Globally, not regionally

Tell us
what you
make.

We are actively looking for manufacturers and suppliers of consumer products — factories with their own production, trading companies with real factory relationships, and sourcing partners who know a category properly.

We do not have a fixed category list, and we are not looking for the cheapest quote. We are looking for partners who can make something well, say honestly what they can and cannot do, and hold quality when an order gets larger than the first one.

If you work with OEM or private-label clients, we are likely a good fit: we build our own brands and our own storefronts, and we need the product underneath to be worth putting a name on.

Type 01

Manufacturers

Own production, OEM or private-label capability, willing to work through samples.

Type 02

Suppliers

Established supply of consumer goods with dependable stock and shipping.

Type 03

Sourcing partners

Agents and specialists with genuine depth in a category and real factory access.

Type 04

Business partners

Operators, logistics and fulfilment partners across the consumer commerce chain.

02 How a partnership starts Short and direct
S—01

You get in touch

Send us what you make, where you make it and who you make it for. A catalogue or a product list is more useful than a company profile.

S—02

We review it against what we are building

We will tell you plainly whether there is a fit right now. If there is not, we will say so rather than leave the conversation open.

S—03

Samples

We buy samples and assess the product the way a customer will — unboxing it, using it, and filming it. Quite a lot is decided here.

S—04

Terms

Minimums, lead times, pricing, packaging and shipping. We would rather have an uncomfortable conversation early than a surprise later.

S—05

A first run, then volume

We start deliberately modest and scale on evidence. If the brand works, order volume moves quickly — which is the point at which we find out what kind of partner we have.

What helps us move quickly
  • Product catalogue or category list
  • Minimum order quantities
  • Production lead times
  • OEM / private-label and packaging capability
  • Relevant certifications or testing
  • Shipping terms and export experience
What we cannot share
  • Current brands, stores or product lines
  • Categories we are actively developing
  • Existing supplier and partner names
  • Commercial performance

We protect our partners' information the same way. Under a signed agreement we are able to go considerably deeper.
